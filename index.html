About the book

“To know a godly father is to glimpse the heart of God.”

The Father Through the Eyes of the Son is a deeply personal memoir that honours the life, faith, and lasting legacy of Ganes Chetty. Written through the loving lens of his son Ashton, this book explores the quiet strength, unwavering devotion, and gentle wisdom that defined a father's walk with God and his family.

More than a personal tribute, it is an inspiring reflection on fatherhood, spiritual heritage, and the eternal bond between a heavenly Father and His children. Through intimate stories and timeless lessons, readers are invited to rediscover the profound impact of a life lived with purpose, integrity, and faith.

Whether you are seeking encouragement in your own spiritual journey, reflecting on the relationship with your own father, or searching for a story of hope and redemption, this memoir will touch your heart and strengthen your faith.

Witness to a Life of Faith

Discover the themes that weave through every page of this memoir:

  • A Legacy of Faith: How one man's devotion to God transformed generations.
  • Fatherhood Reflected: Lessons on gentle strength, sacrifice, and unconditional love.
  • The Quiet Walk: A story of living out faith in everyday moments, trials, and triumphs.
  • The Eternal Bond: Exploring the relationship between earthly fathers and the Heavenly Father.
